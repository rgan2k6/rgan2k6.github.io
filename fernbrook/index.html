Three ways we work

Most clients start with a full design-and-build. Some come to us with a plan in hand, or a finished landscape that needs a steady caretaker.

Design + Build

From the first walkthrough to the last plant in the ground. Site survey, planting plan, hardscape drawings, and construction by our own crew.

Typical scope: $60k–$400k

Build from your plan

Already working with a landscape architect? We price, sequence, and build from their drawings — and flag anything the site disagrees with before it costs you.

Typical scope: $40k–$250k

Estate care

Seasonal pruning, bed renovation, stone repair, and drainage upkeep for landscapes we built — and a few we wish we had.

Year-round, by contract

How a project runs

1

Walk the site

We meet on your property, talk through how you actually use it, and take levels, light, and drainage readings. You get honest feedback on scope and budget the same day.

2

Draw the plan

Scaled plan drawings, plant schedules, and material samples you can hold. We revise until the plan is yours, then price it line by line.

3

Build it once

Our crew builds in sequenced phases with a posted schedule. One foreman, one point of contact, no subcontractor roulette.
