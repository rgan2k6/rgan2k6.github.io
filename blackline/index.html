Ceramic coating, minus the sales pitch

It's not a force field. Here's what it actually does and doesn't do.

What it does

Bonds a hard, slick layer to corrected paint. Water sheets off, road grime doesn't stick, UV fade slows dramatically, and washing takes half the time for years.

What it doesn't

It won't stop rock chips or door dings, and it isn't an excuse to use the tunnel wash. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you the wrong thing.

Why correction comes first

A coating locks in whatever is under it — including swirls. We correct the paint first so you're sealing in a finish worth keeping.

How it lasts

Wash it right and come by once a year for a free inspection and top-up. Our oldest coatings are five years in and still beading.
