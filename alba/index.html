Three rules we work by

Assessment before treatment

We photograph, map, and grade your skin before recommending anything. Plans are written down and given to you.

The conservative dose wins

Results should read as rest and good genetics. If a treatment risks looking like a treatment, we scale it back.

Maintenance is the product

Most of your result comes from what happens between visits. Every plan includes an at-home protocol that costs less than you expect.

Your first visit

  1. Consultation, 45 minutes

    Skin history, imaging, and an honest conversation about goals. $75, credited toward your first treatment.

  2. A written plan

    You leave with a sequenced plan — treatments, timing, and total cost — so nothing is ever a surprise at checkout.

  3. Treat and reassess

    We re-photograph at each milestone and adjust. When your skin holds, visits get further apart, not closer together.
